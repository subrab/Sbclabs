Case Studies

Selected work — the business story, not just the build.

A look at products SBC Labs has delivered: the business problem, the solution, and what changed for the client.

Finance / Lending

Digital Lending & Collection Platform

A lending and collection management platform designed to replace manual loan and EMI tracking workflows.

SBC Labs' Role: Product Ownership · Solution Architecture · Full‑stack Development
Business Challenge

Loan and EMI tracking handled through manual, disconnected records — slow to update, hard to audit.

Product Opportunity

A single system for loan creation, repayment schedules and collection status, visible to the whole team.

Solution

Loan creation, automated EMI schedule generation, payment collection, balance tracking, customer management, document generation and a live dashboard.

Outcome

Loan and EMI status moved off scattered spreadsheets into one live system the operations team checks daily — collection status is now visible in one place instead of being reassembled from multiple files.

Catering / Global Brand

Annam Global — Digital Presence

Digital transformation of a traditional catering business into a modern global digital presence.

SBC Labs' Role: Product Strategy · Design · Development
Business Challenge

A trusted catering business with no digital presence to match its reach — inquiries relied entirely on word of mouth.

Product Opportunity

A brand website that presents the founder's story, menu and services credibly to a global audience.

Solution

Brand website with global positioning, menu and service presentation, founder story, and a structured customer inquiry flow.

Outcome

The business now has a single web presence that represents the brand consistently to a global audience, with a structured inquiry flow replacing word-of-mouth as the only path in.
